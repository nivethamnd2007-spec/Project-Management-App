Simple project management for everyone

TaskFlow helps you plan projects, track progress and meet deadlines, all from one clean dashboard that runs right in your browser.

Go to Dashboard View Projects

Our Purpose

Many students, freelancers and small teams keep their projects in notes, chats or spreadsheets, and important deadlines get lost. TaskFlow gives them one simple place to store every project, see what is in progress, and know what is finished. It needs no installation, no server and no sign-up fees: your data is saved securely in your own browser using Local Storage.

Main Features

Project Management

Add, view, edit and delete projects with a name, description, dates and status.

Live Dashboard

See total, in-progress, completed and pending projects at a glance.

Search & Filter

Find any project instantly by name or description and filter by status.

User Accounts

Sign up, log in and keep your own private list of projects and profile details.

Dark & Light Mode

Pick the theme you like. Your choice is remembered every time you come back.

Fully Responsive

Works smoothly on desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
